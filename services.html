What we do

Plumbing work, done by people who explain it as they go.

Every job below is something we handle regularly around Kansas City — from a dripping faucet to a full repipe.

Drain cleaning & repair

Slow kitchen and bathroom drains, main line clogs, and recurring backups. We find out why a drain keeps clogging, not just clear it for the week — using cameras when a blockage isn't obvious from the fixture.

Water heaters

Leak diagnosis, tank and tankless repair, replacement, and installation. If your unit is still under manufacturer warranty, we can work through that process with you rather than starting from scratch.

Pipe repair & repiping

Cracked cast iron, corroded galvanized supply lines, and pinhole leaks. For pipes in hard-to-reach spots, we use trenchless methods like fiberglass pipe lining so we're not opening up an entire wall or slab.

Emergency plumbing

Burst pipes, sewer backups, no hot water, and anything that can't wait until Monday. Someone answers the phone at any hour, and we prioritize jobs that are actively causing damage.

Fixtures & faucets

Leaky or broken faucets, running toilets, outdoor spigots, and garbage disposals — repaired or replaced, including fixtures you've already bought and want installed correctly.

Gas & appliance lines

Gas range, dryer, and water heater hookups. For condos and apartments, we're used to coordinating approvals and documentation with building management so the job isn't held up on paperwork.

Not sure which of these fits your problem?

Describe it on the phone and we'll tell you straight — no charge just to talk it through.

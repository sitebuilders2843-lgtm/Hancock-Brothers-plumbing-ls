Who we are

A local, family-run crew — not a call center.

When you call, you get someone who knows the job and can talk you through it in plain language.

Built around showing up

Hancock Brothers Plumbers is a Kansas City plumbing company built on a simple idea: answer the phone, show up when you say you will, and explain what's actually wrong before touching anything. Customers tell us again and again that the thing they remember isn't just the repair — it's a technician who took the time to walk them through it, whether that's a coordinated fix in a high-rise condo or a Saturday emergency after a DIY project goes sideways.

We keep the crew small enough that the people answering calls, giving estimates, and doing the work all know each other — and know the standard we hold each job to.
